### POST 6 — Pillar Card: Healing the Spirit Through the Skin
**Day 10 · Static Graphic · Collab Post: Dara + Julie + Ericka**

Touch is one of the oldest medicines.

Ritual bathing. Anointing oils. The practice of being tended — and of tending yourself. Across traditions, caring for the body has always been understood as spiritual care. Not separate from it. The same thing.

We've inherited that. And in a lot of cases, we've been encouraged to forget it.

On April 19 we're going back to it — what those practices hold, where they come from, and what it looks like to bring them into a life that's genuinely yours.

This is the third of three conversations. All three are worth having.
