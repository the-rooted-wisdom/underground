01 · Campaign Concept

The Spirit of This Workshop

Beauty as a practice, not a performance. Skin as a living record. Julie and Ericka bring their own depth to this dialogue, and together we'll explore what it means to tend to ourselves from the inside out.

"Skin is not just a surface. It is a record, a threshold, and a place of meeting."

Campaign Voice Anchor · Beauty and the Skin

Healing Skin Conditions

The skin speaks. Breakouts, dryness, sensitivity, inflammation — these are not flaws to correct but communications to understand. We'll work with herbs that have long been used to support the skin in its own language.

  • Reading what the skin is expressing
  • Anti-inflammatory and vulnerary herbs
  • Plant-based formulas for skin healing
  • Ancestral skincare traditions

Healing Through the Skin

The skin is also a doorway in. Topical herbs can move into the body — easing muscle aches, releasing cramps, soothing deep tension. What we apply on the outside does not stay there.

  • Transdermal herb absorption
  • Poultices, salves, and liniments
  • Muscle aches, cramps, and tension
  • Circulatory and lymphatic support

Healing the Spirit Through the Skin

Touch is one of the oldest medicines. Ritual bathing, anointing, anointed oils — across traditions, tending the body has always been a form of spiritual care. We'll explore what that looks like in practice.

  • Ritual skin care as spiritual practice
  • Aromatic and nervine herbs
  • Bathing and anointing traditions
  • The body as sacred ground

07 · Usage Notes

How to Apply This System

This campaign palette is a sub-expression of the main Rooted Wisdom identity. It inherits the typographic system and spatial rules entirely — only the colors shift. When in doubt, let the main brand guide lead; this guide handles only the workshop-specific overrides.

What Carries Over from Main Brand

  • Cormorant Garamond + Jost typefaces
  • Eyebrow tracking (0.38–0.42em)
  • Diamond ◈ ornament
  • Watercolor pool blur technique
  • Paper grain texture overlay
  • Spacing and section structure
  • 3px border-radius on cards

What Changes for This Campaign

  • Background: parchment → blush parchment
  • Pool fills: blue tones → petal + mauve
  • CTA color: rust → rose
  • Eyebrow color: blue-wc3 → mauve
  • Display italic: rust → rose
  • Footer bg: bark → bark-rose
  • Body text: mid-text → mid-rose

Presenter Credit Rule

Dara, Julie, and Ericka share equal visual weight in all materials. Always list all three names. The preferred order is Dara Pressley · Julie · Ericka. On social media, condense to @handles when format requires. Never lead with just one name on campaign-facing materials.

CTA Language

  • Register for Bloom & Tend
  • Reserve Your Seat
  • Join Us · April 19
  • Register at therootedwisdom.com

Plain uppercase text, not button pills, for social. Standard button styling on web.
